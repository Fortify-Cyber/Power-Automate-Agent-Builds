Microsoft update · November 2, 2026

What's new in Copilot for October: Word design help, Teams recaps and more

Microsoft's October update brings several changes Copilot users at the firm can use now, plus two that arrive in November.

What's new

  • Word, legal skills: more than 20 legal skills for drafting from precedents, reviewing contracts against a playbook and redlining. Available now. See our earlier post.
  • Word, document design: when Copilot drafts a document, it now applies structure, layout and formatting suited to the type of document. Available now.
  • Teams, meeting recaps without a transcript: organizers with Copilot can turn on an AI summary of decisions and action items without keeping a transcript after the meeting. Available now. Follow the firm's policies on recording and transcribing meetings.
  • OneDrive, Prompt Gallery: ready-made prompts for common file tasks. Available now.
  • Coming in November: saved, reusable skills and Projects (keeping related chats, files and instructions together) arrive in Copilot Chat.

Try it

  1. In OneDrive, select the Copilot button in the bottom-right corner.
  2. Choose Open Prompt Gallery and browse the Create, Analyze, Find and Collaborate categories.
