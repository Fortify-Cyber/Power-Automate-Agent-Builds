# Your Daily Digest ☀️

**Daily Digest** is a Copilot agent that reads your inbox, Microsoft To Do and Planner and tells you, in one short briefing, what needs your attention today:

- 🔴 **Needs attention now**: urgent requests, approvals, deadlines, and ⚠️ suspicious payment or credential requests
- 📨 **Waiting on your reply**: emails addressed to you that ask a question or need a response (threads you've already replied to are skipped)
- ✅ **Tasks**: overdue, due today, and due this week
- 👀 **For awareness**: everything else, kept brief

It only sees **your** mail and tasks, it can't send or delete anything, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

## Try it now (1 minute)
1. Open **Microsoft 365 Copilot** (in Teams, Outlook, or at [m365.cloud.microsoft/chat](https://m365.cloud.microsoft/chat)).
2. Select **Daily Digest** from the agents list on the left (or type `@Daily Digest`).
3. Select **Run my daily digest**.
4. The first time, select **Allow** when asked to connect to Outlook, To Do and Planner.

## Get it every morning automatically (30 seconds)
1. In the Daily Digest chat, send **Run my daily digest**.
2. Hover over your message and select **Schedule this prompt** (the clock icon).
3. Choose **Every weekday** at a time that suits you, e.g. **7:30 AM**.
4. Turn on **Email me when the response is ready** if you'd like an email notification.

> Don't see the schedule option in the agent chat? Go to **Copilot Chat**, type `@Daily Digest run my daily digest`, and schedule that prompt instead.

## Handy follow-ups
- "Draft a reply to #2"
- "Tell me more about the Contoso email"
- "Run my digest for everything since Friday"
- "What's overdue?"

## Tips
- Flag an email in Outlook, or add a task in To Do or Planner, and it will appear in tomorrow's digest.
- The digest is AI-generated. Glance at the linked email before acting on anything important, and **always verify payment or bank-detail changes by phone**.
- Feedback? Tell your IT team what the digest missed or what felt like noise, and they can tune it.
